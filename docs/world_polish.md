# Day, night, and world details

## Lighting cycle

`project/scripts/directional_light_3d.gd` controls a 20-minute cycle: 13 minutes between 06:00 and 18:00 and 7 minutes between 18:00 and 06:00. It starts at 08:00, respecting the configured `hora` value. `duracao_dia` represents the full cycle and `proporcao_dia` controls the daylight fraction; both are editable in the Inspector. `set_hour()` allows immediate preview of a time of day.

The sun is above the terrain at noon and stops illuminating it at night. Ambient light changes from 0.45 during the day to 0.075 at night, with a cool tint. A weak moonlight (energy 0.045) provides minimal orientation without a second shadow render. Sky and fog follow the lighting, with warm transitions at the horizon. Water follows `world_daylight` so it does not remain bright because of its unshaded shader. The environment is duplicated to avoid changing shared resources. Visual parameters update at 10 Hz.

## Textures and vegetation

Iron and diamond ores have their own rock textures with inclusions rather than reusing stone with a uniform tint. Oak logs use `oak_log_side` for the bark and `oak_log_top` for the top and bottom, with growth rings. The new plants are daisies, cornflowers, poppies, short grass, and ferns. Textures remain 32 × 32 RGBA in the atlas generated by Block Registry. Existing IDs are preserved; the new plants use IDs 17–21.

Sources and prompts for generated assets are recorded in `project/art/texture_prompts.json`. The PNGs used by the game are in `project/textures/blocks/`. The icon cache automatically invalidates images when imported textures or the registry change.

Regenerate the registry, C++ IDs, and atlas after changes:

```sh
XDG_DATA_HOME=/tmp/godot-polish-tests godot --headless --path project --script res://scripts/tools/rebuild_block_assets.gd
scons -j4
```

Test day/night:

```sh
XDG_DATA_HOME=/tmp/godot-polish-tests godot --headless --path project --log-file /tmp/godot-day-night.log --script res://tests/day_night_test.gd
```

Use the inventory to visually compare the new ores and logs. Create a new world to assess the distribution of new plants without old edits. Darkness and colors still depend on the monitor and renderer.

Deterministic plant-distribution test:

```sh
python3 tests/run_tests.py --filter surface_vegetation
```

Validation performed: editor import, atlas and ID generation, SCons build, lighting cycle, plant distribution across three seeds, texture dimensions/transparency, and icon integration with the inventory. Lighting tests use headless Godot; they do not measure the final appearance in a session with graphical rendering.
